Map of eastern Canada and the northeastern United States showing the St. Lawrence large-ship route as one continuous band from Lake Ontario past Montreal and Québec City, through the estuary and the Gulf of St. Lawrence, to Cabot Strait and the Atlantic. Colors show the source of each part: our AIS routes on Lake Ontario and the upper river, World Bank density from Montreal to Québec City, and Canadian density from Québec City to the sea. Northern Maine lies about 55 to 100 km south of the estuary.
