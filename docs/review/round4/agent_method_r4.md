# Round-four blind review: method and code (R4MET)

Reviewed: `paper_rev486.md` (doc revision 486) against the repository as checked
out on November 14, 2026 (branch `claude/shipping-zone-rev1`, HEAD `67d1542`).
MATLAB was read, not run. Python tests and three scratch checks were run; no repo
file was changed.

## Summary

I traced every method claim in the paper (filter tests, thresholds, year union,
port-approach rule, tug-barge rule, charted lanes and their equidistance test,
zone distance, water clip with the 200 nautical mile cap, land area, Hawaii and
Alaska ship-days rule, satellite hexagons and cutoff, and the checks) to the
code. Most of them match. The filter, the 4-ship rule with its per-year union,
the port-approach rule, the ship-days rule, the water clip and cap, the
EPSG:6933 land areas, and the sampling calendar all do what the paper says.
Table 1's IMO-step counts come back exactly when I add the main and towing caches
(1,347,288 reports and 2,184 ships on 2024-07-10; 1,101,052 and 2,017 on
2025-07-09). The ship-days cutoff of 6 is the best whole number: I added K = 7,
which the code skips, and it scores lower (Jaccard 0.762, against 0.804 at 6).
**Python tests:** `python3 -m pytest -q python/` gives 9 passed in 0.85 s. Those
9 cover only photo sampling (4) and the carriage-rate estimator (5). pytest
doesn't collect `python/test_known_answer.py`, because it has no `test_`
function. Run as a script, it passes: 37 of 37 lower-48 pairs, 2 of 2 Alaska
pairs, and the planted position jump is flagged. **Findings: 14 in total, with
0 Fix, 5 Should fix, and 9 Note.** No finding changes a 25 km or 50 km land area.
The biggest one (R4MET-1) makes the largest zone distance, 100 km, about half a
grid cell narrower than its name. That cuts roughly 0.3% to 0.5% from the
lower-48 100 km land area (my estimate, not a rerun). Two findings correct the
paper's explanation of a check (R4MET-2, MATLAB and Python counts) or of a
calibration (R4MET-3, the satellite cutoff). I didn't re-raise any item that
rounds 1 to 3 dispositioned, such as in-grid U.S. land only, vertex distances,
or the charted-lane 400 km rule (R3COD-8 to R3COD-10).
