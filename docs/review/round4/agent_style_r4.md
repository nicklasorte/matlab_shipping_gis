## R4STY report: style, clarity, and Section 508 (round 4, revision 486)

**Summary.** I reviewed revision 486 against the Google developer documentation style guide, NTIA conventions, and Section 508. I also viewed five figure images: Figures 1, 2, 4, 5, and A-8. This report has 26 findings: 9 Fix, 13 Should fix, and 4 Note. It leaves out the 34 suggestions already pending from round 2 (R2STY-6 to 8, 12 to 15, 17, 18, 20 to 23, and 25). Round 2 found several problems that are now fixed: the 5.3 heading (R2STY-9), the 1.1 carriage hedge (R2STY-10), the major-ports claim in 1.8 (R2STY-11), the lower-48 land shares (R2STY-16), the 1.10 non-sequitur (R2STY-19), and the A.2 parallel figures (R2STY-24). The largest remaining group of 508 problems is alt text and captions. Five alt texts still give no takeaway. One alt text leaves out what its image labels (Hilo). Two captions describe colors in ways a reader can't tell apart or match to the image. One image calls its data "registry" gross tonnage, but the text calls it Wikidata gross tonnage. Long sentences are the main readability problem. A script counts 87 sentences over 30 words. Six of the longest are already pending, so this report rewrites the three worst of the rest and lists the others. None of these findings changes a route, a zone, or a number.
