**R2OPP review: opposing reader and maritime expert check of the 2.7 GHz large-ship routes paper (doc rev. 329)**

## 1. Summary

The revision answers the mock filing's strongest point. Zones are now measured from the route cells, not from the shoreline (`init_shipping_zone_rev1.m:73`, `zone_mode='ships'`). It also fixes the stability check and reports the AIS error-rate rebuild (the routes rebuilt without flagged reports). Of the 20 rebuttal arguments, 9 are now answered: 1, 2, 4, 5, 17, 18, 19, 20 and 8 (8 was already rebutted). Seven are partly answered: 3, 7, 11, 12, 14, 15 and 16. Four still stand: 6, 9, 10 and 13.

Moving to ships mode makes Alaska's satellite hexagons matter more. By my estimate they now explain about 19% of the Alaska 25 km land zone. The only sensitivity test the paper reports for them was run under the old shore mode.

Most maritime regulatory claims check out against public sources. The exceptions are three overstated or out-of-date statements (on AIS rules, MarineCadastre attribute data and a VTS count) and Mississippi river distances that run about 20 km short at Baton Rouge.

**Status of the 20 rebuttal arguments**
- **Answered (9):** 1 (ships mode), 2 (presence test stated in Sections 1.5 and 3), 4 (Section 1.3 rewritten), 5 (rerun on the 52-day base, both directions, Jaccard 0.76), 8, 17 (Section 3.1 step 3), 18 (disclosed in Section 1.3), 19 (Section 1.6), 20 (coast limit added, −3% without the rule).
- **Partly answered (7):** 3 (the sweep covers only 2 to 8 ships and only the 100 km zone is quoted), 7 (the coarse cutoff search is disclosed, not refined), 11 (the data's age is disclosed, but there is no run without charted lanes), 12 (Hawaii stability added, but the port column is still used as a reason and no zone areas are given under both rules), 14 (the SOLAS Great Lakes exclusion is stated, but the Great Lakes zones aren't flagged as resting on an assumption), 15 (disclosed in Section 1.4 but not in Section 1.6), 16 (caveats and lower-48 areas added, but files are still named by frequency band).
- **Still stand (4):** 6 (Spearman 0.231 is absent), 9 (no run without satellite lanes; worse under ships mode, see R2OPP-1), 10 (the caveat that Kapsar ship fields are entered by hand is absent), 13 (the "Most" share of ships with S-band has no source).

## 3. Checked and found correct

- **SOLAS V:** Regulation 1.1 exclusions (warships, government ships, ships sailing only on the Great Lakes), 19.2.3.2 (9 GHz radar at 300 GT) and 19.2.7.1 (3 GHz radar at 3,000 GT, "or … a second 9 GHz radar") are stated correctly ([MCA copy](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/5a7f0081ed915d74e33f3c6e/solas_v_on_safety_of_navigation.pdf)).
- **33 CFR 164.35** names no band. **164.46(b)(1)(i)** threshold of 65 ft is correct.
- **Coast Guard AIS coverage**, 24 nautical miles two-way and 50 nautical miles receive-only, is correct ([USCG CG-9](https://dcms.uscg.mil/Our-Organization/Assistant-Commandant-for-Acquisition-CG-9/Newsroom/Latest-Acquisition-News/Article/1664712/coast-guard-nais-transitions-to-sustainment)).
- **MarineCadastre metadata:** the one-minute filtering, the use constraint "For coastal and ocean planning", and Alaska being filtered out at the request of the Marine Exchange of Alaska (MXAK) all match ([InPort 73064](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/inport/item/73064)).
- **ENC Direct Marine Transportation layer:** 291 features in the lower-48 grid, five themes kept, and no features in the Hawaii or Puerto Rico grids. I confirmed this by querying the live layer.
- **33 CFR 161.12** lists 12 VTS areas, including Buzzards Bay and Prince William Sound.
- **Mississippi channel:** the 45 ft channel ends at Mile 232.4, so the route ending just above Baton Rouge fits. New Orleans at 156 km is about 1 mile from Mile 95–95.8.
- **Run logs** match every zone area in the paper (all four regions), Table 3 (sum 684,524), the port counts, and the foreign-cell drops (3,427 and 6,800).
- **Stability JSON** matches Section A.6 and Section 4.2 (81%, 93%, 57%, 33%, Jaccard 0.764; Hawaii 63% and 56%).
- **Section 3.1 percentages** (22%, 10%, 4%) match the cell-basis areas in `fix_results_rev1.md:47-50`.
- **Lower-48 charted lanes and racons** add only about 1.8%, 1.1% and 0.6% to the land in the 25, 50 and 100 km zones (paper areas against the sensitivity baseline).

None of the facts I used came from the user's memory.
