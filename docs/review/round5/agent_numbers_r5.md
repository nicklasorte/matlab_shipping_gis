## Summary

Most of the paper's numbers trace cleanly. The land areas for all four regions
match the committed run logs and also reproduce exactly from the committed
`*_land.shp` zone files (I recomputed them in EPSG:6933). Table 8, the population
figures, the PEA sentence, Tables 1 to 6, Table 9, the PSIX towing-vessel tonnage
sentence, the receiver-coverage shares for 2024–2025, Appendix A.20, and Appendix B
all check out, and so do the derived percentages, shares, and sums. The problems
are in a few places. One Alaska cell count in Section 5.3 is from the run before the
D2 decision (High). The Wikidata check uses two different match sets. Section 1.5
gives two different numbers for the effect of the tug-barge rule on the 25 km zone.
Several Section 3 and Section 5.3 figures come from runs made before the November 14
and November 17 reruns. A group of sensitivity, stability, and held-out results
traces only to files under `docs/review/`, not to `outputs/`, so this blind review
couldn't verify them. A handful of specific values have no committed source at all.
Counts: 1 High, 7 Medium, 7 Low.
