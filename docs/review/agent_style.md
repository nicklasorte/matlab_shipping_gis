# Style and Section 508 review

Reviewer: style (AI agent). Inputs: `docs/review/paper_under_review.md` (cited as "paper Lnnn"), `docs/2.7GHz_Routes_and_Zones_Sections_DRAFT.docx` (cited as "docx ¶n", the body-element index from python-docx), and the PNGs in `docs/viz_python`. Scratch scripts are in `/tmp/agent_scratch/style/`.

## Summary

The Word copy fails the marking rule. It has no header or footer parts at all, so DRAFT//PRE-DECISIONAL appears only once, near the top of page 1 (docx ¶2). Every later text page is unmarked. Fix this before the paper goes anywhere. The structure is otherwise sound: all 9 tables have a real header row set to repeat, all 56 images have alt text and sit inline, and the heading levels don't skip. The main 508 problem is color contrast. The light blue `#B4C2E6` (1.78:1 on white) is the main data color in the Mississippi profile and all 15 corridor figures. The outer zone tiers on the Alaska and Hawaii zone maps (`#E4E9F6`, 1.21:1) and the route cells on the Hawaii and Puerto Rico maps (1.4–2.9:1) also fail the WCAG 3:1 minimum for graphics. White labels on `#5674C4` tiles come in at 4.48:1, just under the 4.5:1 minimum for text. On style, the Word captions describe the figures but don't state a takeaway. Most appendix figures and Tables 2, 3, and 7 are not cited before they appear. Nine abbreviations are never spelled out, including SOLAS, IMO, MMSI, NOAA, and VTS. Headings mix title case and sentence case. Range dashes and number formats are inconsistent. I also found five internal number conflicts, most notably where the Mississippi route ends above Baton Rouge (35 km in one place, 5 km in another).
