# Data-use review (agent: datause)

**This is a first-pass data-use review by an AI agent. It isn't a legal
opinion.** Items marked "Needs counsel" need a decision by NTIA counsel (and,
where noted, a security or release reviewer) before the paper or repository is
treated as cleared.

## Summary

The paper and code draw on public sources whose published terms allow the
planning use the paper makes of them. The problems are in what the public
repository republishes and in incomplete attribution. The repository is public
(`gh api repos/nicklasorte/matlab_shipping_gis` returns `"visibility":"public"`,
`"license":null`). It holds 4,524 day caches that pair 1 km cells with ship MMSIs
for each sampled day. Of these, 1,248 cover Alaska in 2018–2019, which is a region
MarineCadastre now filters out of its products at the request of the Marine
Exchange of Alaska (MXAK), the owner of most Alaska receiving stations. The caches
also hold daily positions for 124 military and law enforcement ships (AIS types
35 and 55), and a committed vessel list names 18,212 pleasure craft and sailing
vessel MMSIs that the analysis doesn't need. The Kapsar et al. CC BY 4.0
attribution lacks the title, the full creator list, the publisher, a license link,
and a statement of changes. The paper's reference list (A.21) also leaves out the
Kapsar dataset, NOAA AIS Vessel Transit Counts, and the 2018–2019 MarineCadastre
files. The land areas come from the Census 1:500,000 cartographic boundary file,
whose terms say not to use it for area calculation. The draft paper, marked
DRAFT//PRE-DECISIONAL, is itself in the public repository. The Zone, Number, and
Wording scale doesn't measure legal risk, so the table ranks findings by data-use
risk first and tags them "Needs counsel" where that applies.

## Checked and found correct

- **Kapsar license and DOI.** The hexagon package is CC BY 4.0
  (`data/ref/kapsar_hex/hex.xml`, `intellectualRights`). DOI 10.18739/A2XG9FC41 is
  the hexagon data that the code reads (PMC article specifications table,
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9436749).
- **Kapsar privacy.** The authors de-identified and aggregated the data under
  exactEarth's privacy policy (`hex.xml` "Ethical Research Practices"). The
  paper uses only hexagon aggregates, so it adds no ship identity.
- **No implied endorsement.** Nothing in the paper suggests that the Kapsar
  authors endorse the study (CC BY 4.0 Section 2(a)(6)).
- **MarineCadastre terms fit the use.** The access constraints are "None" and
  the use constraint is "For coastal and ocean planning," which fits a spectrum
  planning study. Section 1.10 already notes that the data aren't for navigation
  (https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/inport/item/73064).
- **Census acknowledgement.** Both Census use constraints require acknowledgement
  of the Census Bureau, and the paper gives it in Section 1.2 and A.21. TIGER/Line
  files aren't copyrighted (`tl_2023_us_coastline.shp.iso.xml`).
- **ENC Direct.** The access constraints are "None," and the paper's planning use
  fits the stated purpose (https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/inport/item/39973).
- **Principal Ports license.** The data.gov record lists CC0 1.0
  (https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/principal-ports).
- **Wikidata.** Wikidata structured data are CC0, which needs no attribution, and
  the paper credits Wikidata anyway. This is unverified in this session: the
  licensing page, https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing, couldn't be
  fetched.
- **Caches limited to large ships.** All day caches use the 100 m length filter
  (`L100` in each file name), so they hold no small-craft MMSIs. The pleasure
  craft are only in the vessel list (datause-5).
- **Aggregate outputs hold no IDs.** The QC JSON files (`outputs/*/qc/*.json`) and
  the Alaska outputs (`alaska_daily.csv`, `alaska_rule_cells.npz`,
  `satellite_lane_cells.csv`) hold counts or cell indices only, with no MMSIs.
- **Figure credit.** The Alaska figure source line already credits Kapsar and
  CC BY 4.0 (`python/ship_alaska_figures.py:34-36`). It still needs the title,
  the link, and the changes made (datause-6).
