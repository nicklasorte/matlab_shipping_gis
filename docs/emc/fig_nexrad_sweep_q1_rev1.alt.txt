Two line charts of separation distance against NEXRAD tuned frequency from 2800 to 3100 MHz, both antennas scanning, interference no more than 1% of the time, with each ship radar at its closest tuning. Left, ship radar into NEXRAD at NEXRAD's pulsed protection level: magnetron radars need 18 km at 2800 MHz and 224 km at 3050 MHz; the solid-state radar needs 72 km and 404 km. Right, NEXRAD into ship radar at -6 dB: 61 km and 480 km (magnetron), 71 km and 608 km (solid state). Dashed lines show the other protection level. A shaded band marks the magnetron tuning range, 3020-3080 MHz, and a dotted line marks 3000 MHz, the top of NEXRAD's published range.
