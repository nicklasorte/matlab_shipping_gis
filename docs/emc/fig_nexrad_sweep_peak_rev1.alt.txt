Two line charts of separation distance for 50% of the time against NEXRAD tuned frequency from 2800 to 3100 MHz, both beams aimed along the path, NEXRAD at its lowest tilt seen along the horizon ray, with each ship radar at its closest tuning. Left, ship radar into NEXRAD at NEXRAD's pulsed protection level: magnetron radars need 58 km at 2800 MHz and 523 km at 3050 MHz; the solid-state radar needs 334 km and 781 km. Right, NEXRAD into ship radar at -6 dB: 238 km and 909 km (magnetron), 324 km and 1029 km (solid state). Dashed lines show the other protection level. A shaded band marks the magnetron tuning range, 3020-3080 MHz, and a dotted line marks 3000 MHz, the top of NEXRAD's published range.
