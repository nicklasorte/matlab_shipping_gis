Two line charts of separation distance for 50% of the time against NEXRAD tuned frequency from 2800 to 3100 MHz, main beam to main beam, with each ship radar at its closest tuning. Left, ship radar into NEXRAD at NEXRAD's pulsed protection level: magnetron radars need 62 km at 2800 MHz and 606 km at 3050 MHz; the solid-state radar needs 394 km and 872 km. Right, NEXRAD into ship radar at -6 dB: 291 km and 1003 km (magnetron), 384 km and 1125 km (solid state). Dashed lines show the other protection level. A shaded band marks the magnetron tuning range, 3020-3080 MHz, and a dotted line marks 3000 MHz, the top of NEXRAD's published range.
